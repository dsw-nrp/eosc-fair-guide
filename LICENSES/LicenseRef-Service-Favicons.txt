Favicons of tools and services

The images in assets/favicons/ are the favicons of the tools and services the guide names, each taken from the
site of the service it identifies and reduced to one small square image by scripts/vendor_favicons.py. The address
each was taken from and the date are recorded in assets/favicons/sources.toml.

They are not covered by the MIT License or by Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. They are shown only beside the
name of the service they belong to, to identify it. All rights remain with their owners, and any other use is
subject to their rules.
