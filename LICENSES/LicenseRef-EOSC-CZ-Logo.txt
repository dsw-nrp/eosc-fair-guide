EOSC CZ logos

The files assets/img/eosc-cz-logo.svg, assets/img/logolink-cs.svg and assets/img/logolink-en.svg are official
logos of the EOSC CZ initiative and the logolink of the European Union and the Ministry of Education, Youth and
Sports of the Czech Republic (MŠMT). They are taken unchanged from the official packages published at
https://www.eosc.cz/o-nas/sablony-eosc-cz.

They are not covered by the MIT License or by Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. They are used to identify the
guide as an output of the EOSC CZ initiative and of the National Repository Platform project, as the
acknowledgement rules at https://www.eosc.cz/o-nas/acknowledgement ask. Any other use is subject to the rules of
their owners.
