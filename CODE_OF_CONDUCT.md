# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone who takes part in the project, in issues, pull requests, reviews and any other project space, is
expected to follow it.

## In short

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in review: comment on the work, not on the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Welcome people of every background and level of experience.
- Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile
(<https://github.com/motyc>). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove, edit or reject
contributions, and may block participants, in line with the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor
Covenant.
