# Guide knowledge base

## Requirements

### Requirement: Practical guidance for human consent, data appraisal, and team handover

The knowledge base SHALL contain explicit operational recommendations addressing informed consent for human participants, criteria for selecting and appraising data for long-term retention, and procedures for ensuring data continuity when research personnel depart.

#### Scenario: Human subjects data planning
- **GIVEN** a researcher designing a study involving human subjects
- **WHEN** consulting Station 1 (Plan)
- **THEN** the guide advises formulating consent agreements and ethics submissions that allow future reuse (in anonymized or controlled-access form) and avoid unnecessary data destruction commitments.

#### Scenario: Data appraisal and selection
- **GIVEN** a researcher with large computational or experimental datasets
- **WHEN** deciding what to deposit in a repository
- **THEN** the guide clarifies that primary observations, underlying data for publications, and scripts must be preserved, while recomputable intermediate data need not be archived long-term.

#### Scenario: Personnel departure and handover
- **GIVEN** research staff or students concluding their work on a project
- **WHEN** consulting Station 2 (Collect)
- **THEN** recommendations provide handover checklists and advise using institutional or e-INFRA shared storage rather than personal local drives.
